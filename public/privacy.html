Booking privacy notice

Updated 14 October 2026.

Who manages your information

Transformation Laser Studio, 754 London Road, Thornton Heath, CR7 6JA, manages the personal information submitted through this booking website. For a booking or privacy enquiry, email zaeemahmad365@gmail.com or call 07719 598265.

What we collect and why

We collect your name, email, phone number, selected treatments, preferred date and time, and any booking notes you provide. We use these details to arrange your appointment, check availability and communicate booking updates. This is necessary to take steps at your request before providing a service and to manage the booking. Please keep notes limited to appointment arrangements; do not enter medical or payment information here.

The studio may add private administrative notes, such as a cancellation reason. These help us manage requests and resolve queries. We do not use this booking form to subscribe you to marketing.

How long website requests are kept

  • Confirmed appointments and unanswered pending requests: 90 days after the appointment date and time.
  • Denied or cancelled requests: 90 days after the request is declined or cancelled.
  • Rescheduled active requests: the period follows the new appointment date and time.

Automatic cleanup removes the request and its stored notes after the retention period. Cleanup runs when booking records are accessed or a new request is saved; a daily cleanup schedule can also run when configured. We may delete unnecessary records sooner. Existing records receive a seven-day review period when the policy is first applied.

Where a record is needed for an unresolved complaint, dispute or legal obligation, we keep the necessary information for that purpose and review it when the issue is resolved. The ordinary 90-day period applies again once the hold is removed, so a record already past that period becomes eligible for deletion.

Storage, emails and other records

The website uses Vercel hosting, private database storage and an email provider to operate the booking service. Authorised studio staff can access booking details through a protected admin page. Customer emails do not include private internal notes or hold reasons.

Deleting a website request does not erase copies already sent by email, downloaded as a spreadsheet or included in provider backups. These copies require separate retention and deletion controls. Backup copies may remain until the provider's backup period ends. Treatment, consent and financial records are held separately and follow their own applicable retention requirements.

Your choices and rights

Contact the studio if you would like to access or correct your details, request deletion or restriction, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. These rights depend on the circumstances; some information may need to be kept to meet legal obligations or resolve a dispute. You can also raise a concern with the Information Commissioner's Office.

This notice covers website booking requests. The website does not require customers to create an account. A necessary session cookie is used when studio staff sign in to Admin.

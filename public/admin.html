90-day booking cleanup

Confirmed and unanswered pending requests are removed 90 days after the appointment time. Denied or cancelled requests are removed 90 days after closing. Each request shows its removal date.

Use Edit to keep a request for an unresolved complaint or dispute. Older requests already on the website get seven days for review before their first removal. Emails and downloaded exports need separate cleanup.
